Welcome to Digestion Junction! A game to learn about the digestive system. There are two sections to this game, the first involves identifying the different enzymes and fluids the digestive system secretes. The second section (WIP) has you complete tasks to get food all the way from the mouth and through the entire digestive system! 

Why are we making this:
We're making this game under our upcoming game studio, The Grovve. We make educational games for students to learn and revise their school curriculum. Digestion Junction like our other WIP games is made in Godot and uses assets we draw in procreate. Once this game is done, it will feature levels that cover the digestive system unit and be able provide students a way to test their knowledge an engaging manner.

Play the game here: https://the-grovve.itch.io/digestion-junction 

How to Play (solution identification):
- Click on the different flasks to add them to the beaker. Then add starch, protein or fat molecules and see how they react!
- Follow the guide on what each solution does.
- Click on the white boxes and type in what you think the flask contains (lowercase only)
- Press the check button to check if your answer is correct!
- Click on the top left guide button for all the information

How to Play (digestion track, WIP):
- Click on the arrows on the bottom to swap between the solution making and digestion track.
- Mix the solutions that are required based on the information given, then press pour
- Whenever you complete a task, the instructions will change to let you know what to do next
- Repeat until they exit through the end
- The arrows beside the digestion track allow you to move where you are looking at the track
- If molecules get stuck, click on the big orange ball and use it to move the molecules. Click again to put it down.

Thanks for playing and stay tuned for more updates!
